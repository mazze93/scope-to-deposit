Scope to Deposit

Workflow help

From inquiry to kickoff

  1. Review scope. Draft proposed work from a sample inquiry. Check each quoted fact and confirm the repository, scope approver, and current CI baseline. Approving the review creates the version the buyer will accept.
  2. Buyer approval. Open the buyer review link. The buyer reads the agreed work and accepts that version.
  3. Confirm access. Record a reference showing that the operator has the repository access needed to deliver the scope.
  4. Settle the deposit. Create a $625 USD PayPal Sandbox invoice and approve making it payable through the share link. The buyer pays through PayPal. Verified payment events update the decision.

The divider gauge counts completed requirements. Viewing a stage moves its navigation marker. A partial payment leaves the deposit requirement pending, and the payment bar shows the amount PayPal reports.

Kickoff becomes ready when scope acceptance, access, and the exact settled deposit are confirmed.

Sandbox setup

Use synthetic inquiries and PayPal Sandbox accounts. Configure the local app credentials, webhook registration, and separate webhook listener using the repository instructions.
