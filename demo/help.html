Workflow help

From inquiry to kickoff

  1. Contact. Save the buyer's name, organization, and Sandbox email.
  2. Reviewed scope. AI proposes work and quotes source facts. The operator resolves missing facts and records the next step and contact point. These become part of the version the buyer accepts.
  3. Buyer acceptance. Share the private buyer link. Acceptance records the exact reviewed version. Link possession identifies the accepting session; it is not independent identity verification.
  4. Invoice delivery. Create the $625 USD Sandbox invoice for the saved buyer. Approve sending it through PayPal. The event records PayPal's accepted send operation; it does not prove the buyer read an email.
  5. Verified payment. The app verifies the webhook signature and fetches the invoice. Partial, pending, manual, refunded, mismatched, and duplicate payments cannot satisfy the full-deposit gate. Confirm repository access with an evidence reference.
  6. Kickoff confirmation. Once all requirements pass, confirm kickoff. Both screens show the buyer, accepted scope, payment receipt, next step, and contact point.

The divider gauge counts completed requirements. Viewing a stage moves its navigation marker. A partial payment leaves the deposit requirement pending, and the payment bar shows the amount PayPal reports.

Kickoff becomes ready when scope acceptance, invoice delivery, access, and the exact settled deposit are confirmed. A later refund puts the engagement on hold while preserving the original confirmation in history. Click any folded sheet to revisit it. Reduced-motion preferences disable unfolding animation.

Sandbox setup

Use synthetic inquiries and PayPal Sandbox accounts. Use the local operator app with Ollama, connected to the Prisma service. Prisma Compute serves buyer links and the webhook; Prisma Postgres stores the engagement and append-only transition history. A standalone SQLite mode remains available for local development.
